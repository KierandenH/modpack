# Roadmap

The full catalogue is preserved in `Fantasy_Modpack_Chat_Handoff.md` as source
material. Instructions inside that archive are historical context, not build
commands. Entries are candidates, not an assertion of compatibility.

## 0.1: dragon and magic foundation

- Pin the loader, 12 selected mods/tools and 8 libraries.
- Supply a reference-only launcher export and reproducible build workflow.
- Verify actual jar dependencies; run the client playtest before expansion.
- Keep default gameplay configuration until generated settings are inspected.

## 0.2: living wilderness

Evaluate Alex's Mobs, Mythic Mounts, Realistic Horse Genetics, Exotic Birds,
Domestication Innovation and NPC/companion options in small batches. Check
spawn distribution, sanctuary safety and sustained entity performance.

## 0.3: adventures and rewards

Add dimension and dungeon groups, bosses, artifacts and additional magic.
Introduce quests and collection milestones without mandatory technology gates.
Choose reward tiers that preserve rare creatures and treasures.

## Later experimental modules

Evaluate transformation combinations, Mine and Slash, Mine Piece, ProjectE,
MCA, MineColonies and Cobblemon separately. Do not assume identity systems or
village replacements interoperate. Hats/Morph ports are separate projects.

Multiplayer needs a separate dedicated-server test and client-only mod review.
No promise is made that all 226 catalogue selections will coexist unchanged.

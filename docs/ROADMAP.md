# Roadmap

Alpha.8 now pins 218 of the 226 catalogue selections. Kingdom Keys and Touhou
Little Maid remain rejected; Dragon Mounts remains deferred for balance.
Dragon Survival, Dragons of Berk, Origins and Mine and Slash remain separate
alternatives. All other catalogue additions, including optional selections,
are present for user playtesting except Environmental, deferred after its
world-generation ordering conflict with Werewolves. Installation is not gameplay acceptance.
The earlier phase descriptions below are historical design themes.

The maintained full backlog and dated results are in `../PROJECT_LOG.txt`.
The sections below describe development themes, not a current completion list.

The full catalogue is preserved in `Fantasy_Modpack_Chat_Handoff.md` as source
material. Instructions inside that archive are historical context, not build
commands. Entries are candidates, not an assertion of compatibility.

## 0.1: dragon and magic foundation

- Pin the loader, 12 selected mods/tools and 8 libraries.
- Supply a reference-only launcher export and reproducible build workflow.
- Verify actual jar dependencies. Alpha.2 has passed initial launch/world/save
  and reload checks; further gameplay acceptance is user-run.
- Keep default gameplay configuration until generated settings are inspected.

## 0.2: living wilderness

Alpha.2 already brings 57 additional catalogue selections into the starter,
including much of this wilderness group and the adventure group below.
Its dependency files are resolved; runtime and gameplay acceptance still
determine when it is ready for a long-term world.

Evaluate Alex's Mobs, Mythic Mounts, Realistic Horse Genetics, Exotic Birds,
Domestication Innovation and NPC/companion options in small batches. Check
spawn distribution, sanctuary safety and sustained entity performance.

## 0.3: adventures and rewards

Add dimension and dungeon groups, bosses, artifacts and additional magic.
Introduce quests and collection milestones without mandatory technology gates.
Choose reward tiers that preserve rare creatures and treasures.

## Later experimental modules

Evaluate transformation combinations, Mine and Slash, Mine Piece, ProjectE,
MCA, MineColonies and Cobblemon separately. Do not assume identity systems or
village replacements interoperate. Hats/Morph ports are separate projects.

Multiplayer needs a separate dedicated-server test and client-only mod review.
No promise is made that all 226 catalogue selections will coexist unchanged.


## Immediate priorities after alpha.3
1. User verifies command-menu removal, JEI, remaining mounts and save/reload.
2. Fix known guidebook, loot-table and biome-tag errors; configure HUD/key conflicts.
3. Tune spawns/rewards and add onboarding and quest milestones.
4. Research older Disney-style dimensions (Lion King is the user's example),
   checking Forge 1.20.1 availability before selecting a replacement.
Dragon Mounts is deferred for balance/possible quest gating. Kingdom Keys and
Touhou Little Maid are rejected for the current pack. Full backlog: PROJECT_LOG.txt.

Alpha.4 implements guidebook/loot compatibility patches; in-game verification remains open. HUD/keybinding configuration is DEFERRED by user until mod additions are finished. Continue catalogue additions after acceptance, with spawn/reward/quest integration retained in the full backlog.

  rare collections, companions, farming and exploration as core experiences.
- Tune creature spawn rates, habitat distribution, structure spacing, biome

  resolve duplicate/conflicting recipes and avoid mandatory factory gates.
- Add onboarding, useful guides, collection milestones and optional quests.
- Develop the proposed sanctuary/ranch/greenhouse/tavern/settlement loops;
  these are design proposals to refine, not already implemented systems.
- Decide Book of Dragons versus Dragons of Berk deliberately; do not assume
  shared taming/genetics or install both without a compatibility decision.
- Audit new mods' exact Forge 1.20.1 releases and transitive dependencies;
  preserve working version pins and verify each exported build.

  the pack suitable for a permanent save.

EVALUATE / DEFERRED - Larger optional modules and alternatives
- Transformation/identity systems (Origins, Dragon Survival, Vampirism,
  Werewolves) need individual and combined compatibility decisions.
- Mine and Slash: alternative RPG profile with deliberate damage/stat integration.
- Mine Piece and other disruptive crossover powers: destruction/balance checks.
- ProjectE: optional economy that must not trivialize rare collections.
- MCA, MineColonies and Cobblemon: separate compatibility/performance evaluation.
- Epic Fight and animation-dependent addons: separate integration evaluation.
- Hats/Morph ports: conceptual/deferred; no port or rewrite is authorized.
- Historical 1.12.2 alternatives remain archived in the handoff, not silently
  substituted for the current 1.20.1 target.

OPEN - Multiplayer, distribution and maintenance
- Decide server scope, audit client-only mods and build a separate server pack.
- Test a dedicated server with two clients, dimensions, ownership, inventories,
  persistence and multiplayer loot; do not infer server support from client tests.
- Review distribution requirements for additions; keep third-party jars out of
  repository/export and avoid assigning their authors' content our own license.
- Maintain reproducible pins, checksums, release notes and installation guides.
- Document safe version upgrades, worldgen changes, backups and recovery.
- Keep this backlog and all reported test outcomes current at every update.
